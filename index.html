Property registration in Udupi involves khata transfer, stamp duty calculation (based on guidance value notified by Sub-Registrar, Udupi), registration fee, sale deed drafting, witness coordination, and post-registration mutation at the village panchayat or CMC. We handle all of this in-house without any third-party coordination required from you. For agricultural land transactions, we also manage RTC mutation, phani updates, and revenue department filings. Post-registration, we also assist with utility transfer — MESCOM electricity name change, KUWS&DB water connection transfer, and CMC tax record updates.

In coastal Karnataka, the word "site" is what locals call a residential plot — they mean the same thing. A site is a measured land parcel ready for construction. When people search "site for sale Udupi", "site near Manipal", or "site near Mangalore" they are looking for plots approved for building a home. Yes — we have sites for sale in Udupi across Brahmagiri, Katapadi road, Kunjibettu, Shivalli, Bypass road, and Kodavoor. We also list sites near Manipal in Madhav Nagar, Shivapura, and Manipal Hill. Land here is typically measured in cents (1 cent = 435.56 sq ft) and sometimes in guntha (1 guntha = 1,089 sq ft, approximately 2.5 cents). A common local search is "30×40 site for sale Udupi" — this means a 1,200 sq ft plot (approximately 2.75 cents). Before buying any site, we verify BMRDA / BDA approval, panchayat limits, road width, 95 or 09A conversion order, RTC records, and the full title chain.

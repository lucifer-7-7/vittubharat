01 RERA

Does K-RERA apply to this flat?

K-RERA registration is required from the promoter of a real estate project, whether that is apartments or a plotted layout, where the land exceeds 500 square metres or there are more than eight units. It does not apply to a private resale between two individuals, or to a single site sold by its owner. A project that already held a completion certificate before the Act came in is outside it. Where it does apply, search the project before the site visit. The record shows the promoter, approved plans, timeline and any litigation.

Source: Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 with the Karnataka Real Estate Regulation and Development Rules, 2017. Search it free, no login, at rera.karnataka.gov.in.

02 Title

Is the title clean for 30 years?

We read the encumbrance certificate for the last 30 years, the sale deed chain, the khata and the tax receipts. The EC is what surfaces a registered charge such as an old loan or a lien. It records registered documents only, so an unregistered claim or an oral arrangement will not appear in it, which is why the deed chain is read alongside. The EC costs 35 rupees for the first year and 10 rupees for each further year on the state portal.

Source: Kaveri Online Services, Department of Stamps and Registration.

03 Completion

Are the OC and CC in order?

For an apartment we coordinate with the builder's legal team so the occupancy certificate, the completion certificate and the RERA registration are all in place before the sale deed is executed. Without an OC the flat is not legally fit for occupation and resale gets harder.

Verified against the builder's file and the planning authority record.

04 Registration

What will stamp duty and registration cost?

Stamp duty is 5 percent on land, commercial property and resale flats, at every price point. The lower slabs, 2 percent up to 20 lakh and 3 percent from 20 to 45 lakh, apply only to the first sale of a flat by the builder under Article 20(2A) of the Karnataka Stamp Act. Cess and surcharge are added on top of the duty amount. Registration is 2 percent of value, uncapped, for documents executed on or after 31 August 2025. Both are charged on the higher of your sale value or the guidance value.

Source: Karnataka Stamp Act, Article 20(2A), and the Karnataka Registration (Amendment) Act, 2025. Before 31 August 2025 the registration fee was 1 percent capped at 30,000 rupees, and many online calculators still show that. Confirm your own figure on the Kaveri calculator.

What do I pay on top of the flat price?

Stamp duty of 5 percent on a resale flat at any price. The lower 2 and 3 percent slabs apply only to a builder's first sale of a flat up to ₹45 lakh. Add cess and surcharge on the duty, plus 2 percent registration since 31 August 2025.

Can an NRI buy a flat in Udupi?

Yes. Under FEMA an NRI or OCI can buy residential property freely, with no cap on the number of units and no RBI permission needed, as long as payment comes through NRE, NRO or normal banking channels. Agricultural land is a different matter and cannot be bought at all.

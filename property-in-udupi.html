✱ BEFORE YOU PAY

The costs and checks on any Udupi purchase

These are state rules, not ours. Each one links to its source so you can check it yourself.

01 Stamp duty

What are the statutory costs?

Stamp duty is 5 percent on land, commercial property and resale flats, at every price point. The lower slabs, 2 percent up to 20 lakh and 3 percent from 20 to 45 lakh, apply only to the first sale of a flat by the builder under Article 20(2A) of the Karnataka Stamp Act. Cess and surcharge are added on the duty amount. Registration is 2 percent of value, uncapped, for documents executed on or after 31 August 2025. Everything is charged on the higher of your sale value or the guidance value.

Source: Karnataka Stamp Act, Article 20(2A), and the Karnataka Registration (Amendment) Act, 2025. Before 31 August 2025 registration was 1 percent capped at 30,000 rupees, and many calculators still show that. Confirm your figure on the Kaveri calculator.

02 Valuation

Why does guidance value matter?

Guidance value is the minimum value the state fixes for an area, what other states call circle rate. It is the floor for stamp duty, so a deal cannot be registered below it, and registering under it can trigger proceedings under Section 45A of the Karnataka Stamp Act. Values are revised annually, normally with effect from 1 April.

Check it by district, taluk, hobli, village and survey number on the Kaveri portal.

03 Title

What does a title check actually cover?

The encumbrance certificate for 30 years, the sale deed chain, the khata and tax receipts, and for land the RTC and mutation entries. The EC records registered documents only, so an unregistered claim or an oral family arrangement will not show in it. That is why the deed chain and, on rural land, the revenue record are read alongside rather than instead.

EC and certified copies from Kaveri, land records from Bhoomi.

04 Land use

Was the land ever agricultural?

All land in Karnataka is agricultural by default. Section 95 of the Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964 lets the Deputy Commissioner convert it to non-agricultural use, which is why people say DC conversion. Without a valid order there is no khata, no building plan sanction, no bank loan and no legal construction. Orders can be partial, lapsed or conditional, so the order itself gets read, not the word converted.

Karnataka Land Revenue Act, 1964, Section 95, verified against Bhoomi. Rules tightened again in September 2025 under the Karnataka Land Revenue Rules Amendment.

05 RERA

Does K-RERA apply?

It applies to a promoter's real estate project, apartments or a plotted layout, where the land exceeds 500 square metres or there are more than eight units. It does not apply to a private resale between individuals or a single site sold by its owner, and a project that already held a completion certificate before the Act is outside it. Where it applies, search it before the site visit.

Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 with the Karnataka Rules, 2017. Free to search, no login, at rera.karnataka.gov.in.

06 NRI rules

What can an NRI buy in Udupi?

Residential and commercial property, freely. No cap on the number of units and no RBI permission needed, as long as payment comes through NRE, NRO or normal banking channels. Agricultural land, plantation property and farmhouses cannot be bought at all, only inherited. A breach makes the purchase void and the penalty can run to three times the transaction value, so what the record says about classification decides it.

Foreign Exchange Management (Non-debt Instruments) Rules, 2019, Rule 24, read with FEMA Section 13.

✱ QUESTIONS

Property in Udupi, asked often

What does property in Udupi cost?

On listings with us now, a 2BHK flat starts from ₹40 lakh, a 3BHK from ₹65 lakh, an independent house from ₹45 lakh, and a residential site from ₹45 lakh for about 2,400 sq ft. Premium villas and beachfront property run well above ₹1 crore.

What kinds of property can I buy in Udupi?

Apartments, independent houses, villas, residential sites and layout plots, commercial shops and office space, agricultural and plantation land, and builder project units.

Which areas in Udupi should I look at?

Bannanje and Ambalpady for the central city, Manipal for university rental demand, Kuthapady and Bailoor for newer apartment projects, the bypass and Katapadi road for sites, and Kaup, Padubidri and Maravanthe for coastal property.

What will I pay beyond the price?

Stamp duty of 5 percent on land, commercial property or a resale flat, at any price. The lower 2 and 3 percent slabs apply only to a builder's first sale of a flat up to ₹45 lakh. Add cess and surcharge on the duty, plus 2 percent registration since 31 August 2025.

How do I check a property is legally clean?

Encumbrance certificate for 30 years, the sale deed chain, khata and tax receipts, the RTC for land, and the Section 95 conversion order if it was ever agricultural. For a builder project, the K-RERA record, approved plans and occupancy certificate. Our legal team does this before any payment.

Can an NRI buy property in Udupi?

Yes for residential and commercial property, with no cap on units and no RBI permission needed, paid through NRE, NRO or normal banking channels. Agricultural land, plantation property and farmhouses cannot be bought at all, only inherited.

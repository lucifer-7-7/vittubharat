Common questions

What do you charge for home loan assistance?

Nothing. You pay us no fee. Our income comes from the builder or seller side of the transaction. You pay only the bank's own processing fee, directly to the bank.

How is my home loan eligibility calculated?

Each bank reads your income differently. We look at your ITR, salary or business income, existing EMIs, and credit history, then apply each bank's own credit policy to see what that specific bank will sanction. The same profile can get different amounts at different banks, which is why the bank you choose matters.

My loan was rejected by one bank. Can you still help?

Often yes. A rejection usually means that bank's policy did not fit your profile, not that no bank will lend to you. We look at why it was rejected and whether another lender reads the same file differently. If no bank will approve it today, we tell you that and explain what to fix first.

Which banks do you work with in Udupi and Mangalore?

SBI, HDFC, Axis Bank, Bank of Baroda, Canara Bank, Karnataka Bank, and several co-operative banks common in coastal Karnataka.

What documents should I bring to the first meeting?

Bring your last two to three years of ITR, recent salary slips or business bank statements, PAN and Aadhaar, and details of any running loans. If you have already chosen a property, bring its documents too. If you do not have everything, come anyway and we will tell you what is missing.

Do you handle NRI home loans?

Yes. Ajay Pandit handles NRI files directly. The documentation is done by our office, not passed to a third party.
